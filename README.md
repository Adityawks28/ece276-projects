# ECE 276A & 276B Projects

Hello! I am a student of Professor Shumon Koga at Kobe University and a member of the
[CINAPS Lab](https://cinapslab.com/). This repository contains my own reports
and code for projects from two UCSD robotics courses:
[ECE 276A](https://natanaso.github.io/ece276a/index.html) (Sensing & Estimation in Robotics) and
[ECE 276B](https://natanaso.github.io/ece276b/) (Planning & Learning in Robotics).

Course datasets and provided files are not included.

## ECE 276A: Sensing & Estimation

Projects 1 and 2 are included. Project 3 will be added after it is completed.

1. **Project 1: Orientation Tracking** (`ECE276A_PR1/`)
   IMU calibration, quaternion orientation estimation, projected gradient
   descent, and panorama construction.

2. **Project 2: LiDAR Based SLAM** (`ECE276A_PR2/`)
   Wheel and IMU odometry, ICP scan matching, pose graph optimization with
   loop closures (GTSAM), occupancy grid and floor texture maps from RGBD
   images. Report: `ECE276A_PR2/ECE276A_PR2_Report.pdf`.

3. **Project 3: Visual Inertial SLAM (planned)**
   IMU localization, landmark mapping, and visual inertial SLAM using an EKF.

## ECE 276B: Planning & Learning

1. **Project 1: Dynamic Programming** ([`ECE276B_PR1/`](ECE276B_PR1/))
   Door & Key navigation in MiniGrid, formulated as a Markov Decision Process
   and solved with backward dynamic programming, for 7 known maps and one
   policy shared by 36 random maps. Results and GIFs are in the project README;
   the report is in progress.
